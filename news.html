Summer 2026

Building our partner network in Uganda and Kenya

We're expanding our network of vetted, community-led partners across Uganda and Kenya — organizations already trusted in their communities to support children's welfare and education. Our focus is on giving these existing efforts the visibility and reliable funding they need to plan for the long term.

Spring 2026

ELYRA Earth: our transparency platform takes shape

Work is underway on ELYRA Earth, the platform that connects donors directly to specific projects and reports back on what their support achieves. It's the practical engine behind our promise that giving should be traceable from the donor to the community.

6 May 2026

Elysium Earth Global is founded in Oslo

Elysium Earth Global was established in Oslo as a Norwegian non-profit foundation (forening), org.nr 937 760 140. It provides the governance, accountability, and legal home for our mission: bringing radical transparency to charitable giving.
