What we do

We help good local work reach the people who want to fund it.

Across Uganda and Kenya, dedicated community organizations already care for children and run schooling and welfare programs. What they often lack is visibility and reliable funding. That's the gap we close.

The digital village model

Instead of a one-time donation that vanishes, we help build a lasting connection between supporters and a real community project. Think of it as joining a village around the work you care about — you can follow it, fund it, and see it grow over time.

Through our platform, ELYRA Earth, each project gets its own clear profile: what it does, who it serves, and how support is used. Supporters aren't buying a feeling — they're backing a specific effort they can keep watching.

Our focus

  • Children's welfare and protection
  • Access to education and schooling
  • Strengthening existing community organizations
  • Long-term, sustainable support over one-off aid

How we choose and support partners

First

We vet

We work with established, community-led organizations already trusted in the places they serve — not intermediaries.

Then

We connect

We give each partner a clear, honest presence so supporters anywhere can understand and back their work.

Ongoing

We report

We follow contributions through to the project and share what they supported — the core of our transparency promise.

Where we work

Uganda & Kenya

Uganda

We partner with community welfare and education initiatives supporting children and families, giving trusted local efforts the visibility and structured funding they need to plan ahead.

Kenya

We work alongside grassroots, child-focused organizations, helping them tell their story to a wider audience and receive support that reaches the ground.
