About us

We're building the charity we wished existed.

Elysium Earth Global exists so that giving can be traced, seen, and trusted — from the person who donates to the community that benefits.

Our mission

We bring radical transparency to charitable giving. We connect donors with vetted, community-led projects for children and education in East Africa, and we commit to showing how every contribution is used.

Too much generosity disappears into overhead, uncertainty, and reports no one can verify. We believe people give more, and more confidently, when they can see the line between their gift and its effect. Making that line visible is our entire purpose.

How we're structured

Elysium Earth Global is the legal and governance home for our work. It provides oversight and accountability, while the day-to-day connection between donors and projects runs on our platform, ELYRA Earth. The people doing the work on the ground are independent, community-led partners.

Governance

Elysium Earth Global

The registered non-profit. Holds legal standing, sets policy, and is accountable for how funds are handled.

Platform

ELYRA Earth

The transparency platform that links donors to specific projects and reports on outcomes. Currently being established as its own entity.

Delivery

Local partners

Existing community organizations in Uganda and Kenya who run the programs and know their communities best.

Accountability, in practice

Being a registered Norwegian non-profit means we operate under Norwegian law and reporting standards. It also means our registration, purpose, and structure are a matter of public record.
