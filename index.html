Why we exist

Most donors never find out what happened to their gift.

Trust in charitable giving is broken. People want to help, but they rarely see how a donation is spent, who received it, or what changed. That uncertainty stops good people from giving — and lets waste go unnoticed.

Elysium Earth Global was founded to fix that. We build a direct, visible line between a donor and a real community project, and we hold ourselves to publishing what we do with every contribution.

We are a Norwegian non-profit built on a single principle: if you can't show it, you shouldn't ask for it.
