Transparency

If we can't show it, we won't ask for it.

Transparency is the reason we exist — not a badge we added afterward. Here's what that commitment means in practice.

The path a donation takes

When you give, your contribution is received by Elysium Earth Global, the registered non-profit. From there it is directed to a specific, vetted community project through our platform, Elyra Earth. We keep the chain short and legible on purpose: fewer hands, clearer records.

We are committed to being open about the costs of running the platform and the organization, so supporters always understand the difference between what funds operations and what reaches a project.

Our commitments

  • Name our partners and where they work
  • Show how contributions are allocated
  • Be clear about operating costs and fees
  • Publish our governance and registration
  • Report back on what support achieved

How we vet partners

We only work with established, community-led organizations that are already trusted where they operate. We look for a real presence in the community, a clear purpose, and a willingness to be as open as we are. Transparency is a two-way expectation: partners who join us agree to show their work.
